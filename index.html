Settings

Your key and your verse list are saved only on this phone. Get a free key at api.esv.org.

The reference is always read by the phone voice. If the ESV recording can't load, the phone voice reads the verses instead.

For a nicer phone voice on iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, download a voice marked “Premium” or “Enhanced,” then pick it below.

Tip: keep this app open while it plays. Phones usually stop the reading voice when the screen locks, so the app tries to keep your screen on.
